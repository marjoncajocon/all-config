
## 1. Curriculum Data Allocation Blueprint

| Training Phase | Target Game Count | Unique Positions | Core Learning Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 Depth | 20K – 50K | 1M – 2M | Legal moves, basic captures, material safety. |
| 6 Depth | 100K – 200K | 4M – 8M | Double-jumps, basic forks, wall structures. |
| 8 Depth | 200K – 400K | 8M – 16M | Midgame trades, center control, opening lines. |
| 10 Depth | 500K+ | 25M+ | Endgame conversion, advanced sacrifices. |

------------------------------
## 2. Why Depth-4 Dataset Size is Capped

* 
* Rapid Policy Saturation: The shallow action space is small.
* Diminishing Generative Returns: Excess games replicate identical short-term blunders.
* Shallow Horizon Blindness: The engine cannot see deeper tactical combinations.
* Preventing Policy Regression: Over-training at depth 4 solidifies bad habits. [2, 3] 
* 

------------------------------
## 3. Execution Rules for the Depth-4 Phase

* 
* Filter Exact Transpositions: Deduplicate identical board states during generation.
* Maintain High Exploration: Use a 20% random exploration factor (epsilon-greedy).
* Inject Root Noise: Add [Dirichlet Noise](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.01072v1) to vary opening sequences.
* Optimize Data Formats: Store unique positions as dense 64-bit integers. [4, 5]
